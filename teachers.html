Mr Fraction’s Word Problem Express

Learning targets

The site teaches one thing: how to start a word problem. Almost every screen comes before the arithmetic. These are the targets it practises, as student-facing “I can” statements with the Bloom’s Taxonomy level of each.

Overarching target

I can analyze a word problem’s structure — which situation it describes and which quantity is unknown — before I choose an operation.

The core trip

One target per screen, in the order a student meets them.

Worth knowing before you use these

  • The Bloom order is inverted on purpose. Analyze (targets 2–5) comes before Apply (8), and a student cannot skip it. That is the site’s central rule: recognize the structure before you calculate.
  • Remember-level targets are almost absent, also on purpose. The one exception is the vocabulary on the Word Board — and even there, the point is that vocabulary alone is not enough.
  • These are targets the site practises, not targets it assesses. Nothing on the site produces a score, so it gathers no evidence of mastery. To assess a target — 2 or 10 are the most revealing — do it off the site. A good check: can the student redraw the model on blank paper a week later?

Standards alignment

Common Core Standards for Mathematical Practice:

  • MP7 — look for and make use of structure: targets 2–6
  • MP1 — make sense of problems and persevere, including checking the answer: target 9
  • MP3 — critique the reasoning of others: target 10
